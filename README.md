# University Buddy System

Welcome to the **University Buddy System**, a platform designed to pair newer students (freshmen and sophomores) with experienced peers (juniors and seniors) to provide invaluable support throughout their academic journey. Our goal is to make university life easier by offering guidance in course selection, academic advising, tutoring, campus navigation, and more—all while fostering friendships and building strong networks in a supportive environment.

## Key Features

### 1. Interactive Interface  
A user-friendly, engaging interface that encourages sustained use throughout the student’s time at university.

### 2. Profile Creation  
Students and buddies can personalize their profiles to share more about themselves.

**Profile Options:**
- **General:** Choose hobbies (sports, reading, writing, art, etc.)
- **Student Type:** (International, Exchange, Local, Part-Time, Freshman, Sophomore, etc.)
- **Advisor Role:** (Academic, Mental Health, Social, Career, Junior, Senior, Masters, etc.)

### 3. Seamless Virtual Advising  
Built-in video call functionality integrates with Zoom, allowing easy remote advising sessions.

### 4. Login System  
A secure registration and login system that allows users to sign up as a buddy, student, or both, providing personalized access and experience.

### 5. Gamified System  
Encourages participation through a point-based reward system and leaderboards.

- **Points awarded for:**
  - Event participation & attendance
  - Helping students (campus tours, advising sessions, engagement)
  - Positive student reviews
- **Rewards include:**
  - Exclusive perks like free university merchandise
  - Priority registration for university events

### 6. Notifications & Calendar Integration  
Event reminders and buddy meeting updates are synced with student calendars, similar to the Moodle Calendar system.

### 7. Feedback Mechanism  
Students can rate and leave comments on advisors, ensuring continuous improvement through constructive feedback.

## Advantages Over Traditional Systems

- **Simplicity & Engagement:**  
  An intuitive matching system eliminates tedious forms, making the process straightforward and enjoyable.
  
- **Versatile Roles:**  
  Allows users to choose roles based on preferences—mentor, advisor, friend, etc.

- **Gamification:**  
  Keeps users motivated through points, rewards, and leaderboard recognition.

- **All-in-One Platform:**  
  Consolidates chat, video calls, event planning, and scheduling in a single, accessible place.

- **Student-Centric Design:**  
  Developed by students, for students—ensuring it directly addresses the real needs and challenges faced by freshmen.
